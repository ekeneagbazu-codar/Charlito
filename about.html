About Charlito Foods

Rooted in Passion, Served with Love

Founded in the heart of Lagos, Nigeria, Charlito Foods was born out of a deep passion for bringing people together over unforgettable meals. We believe that every great feast begins with a single plate—crafted with authentic flavors, fresh local ingredients, and the comforting essence of a home-cooked meal.

From rich, traditional Nigerian delicacies to contemporary culinary favorites, our kitchen serves as a bridge between nostalgia and modern dining. Whether you are grabbing a quick lunch to fuel your day or gathering with loved ones to create lasting memories, Charlito Foods delivers quality, warmth, and flavor in every bite.
